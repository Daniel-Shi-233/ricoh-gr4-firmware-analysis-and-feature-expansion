# Contributing

New contributions to revisions using the GR IV Project Noncommercial Source License 1.0 must be material you own or have authority to submit under that license. By submitting a contribution for inclusion, you agree that it may be distributed under the current project license; you retain your copyright. A separate commercial license for contributions would require permission from their respective copyright holders.

Before opening a pull request:

- Submit only material you created or have permission to distribute under the current license.
- Do not submit camera firmware, extracted system images, brand artwork, camera readbacks, serial numbers, or private user data.
- Mark device-specific findings with the tested camera model and firmware version. Separate observed results from hypotheses.
- For any camera-writing example, include a backup and readback procedure, and clearly identify unverified behavior.
